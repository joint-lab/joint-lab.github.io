We are delighted to announce that [Milo Trujillo](../../people/milo-trujillo/) has successfully defended his Ph.D. in Complex Systems and Data Science on July 18th. Milo’s dissertation, *“Building a kinder superhighway: Online group behavior driven by platform design and social policy”*, explores how governance models and platform design influence community behavior, with implications for social media, open-source communities, and decentralized systems. Co-advised by [Laurent Hébert-Dufresne](http://laurenthebertdufresne.github.io/) and [James Bagrow](https://bagrow.com/), Milo’s research has contributed significantly to our understanding of the dynamics of online communities.

Milo’s work includes studies on the effects of community bans on Reddit, the rise of alt-tech social media platforms, and comparisons of open-source collaboration on and off GitHub. 

Milo is now a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [CoMM Lab at the Network Science Institute](https://www.networkscienceinstitute.org/commlab/team), where he will continue to explore the intersection of social policy and online community behavior.

Congratulations, Milo, on this incredible achievement, and best of luck in your new role!
