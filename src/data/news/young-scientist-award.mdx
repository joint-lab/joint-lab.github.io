---
title: Laurent Hébert-Dufresne receives the 2026 Young Scientist Award from the German Physical Society
date: 2026-02-24
categories: ['news']
---

Laurent Hébert-Dufresne has been awarded the [2026 Young Scientist Award for Socio- and Econophysics](https://www.uvm.edu/cems/cs/news/international-honor-laurent-hebert-dufresne) from the German Physical Society (DPG). 
Congratulations!
The award recognizes outstanding contributions by early-career physics researchers. 
The award was also [covered by SFI](https://www.santafe.edu/news-center/news/german-physical-society-names-laurent-hebert-dufresne-the-2026-young-scientist-award-winner) and [UVM](https://www.uvm.edu/cems/cs/news/international-honor-laurent-hebert-dufresne).
