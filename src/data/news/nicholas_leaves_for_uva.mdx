date: 2024-08-01

It is with great excitement that we announce the graduation of [Nicholas Landry](../../people/nicholas-landry/), who has completed his tenure as a TGIR Postdoctoral Fellow in the lab. During his time at UVM, Nicholas made [several contributions](https://joint-lab.github.io/people/nicholas-landry/) to the dynamics of contagion spread on complex systems. He helped establish the [XGI](https://xgi.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) as the go-to library for the analysis of higher-order interactions networks in Python, and organized the [CCSS conference](https://ccss-conference.github.io/) in 2023.

Nicholas is now an Assistant Professor of Biology at the University of Virginia, where he will continue his groundbreaking work. His new lab, the [Landry Lab](https://landry-lab.github.io/), is already hiring—so make sure to check out the opportunities available.

Congratulations, Nicholas, and best of luck in this exciting new chapter.
