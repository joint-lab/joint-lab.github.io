We are thrilled to welcome [**Leah Keating**](../../people/leah-keating/) to the lab as a Postdoctoral Associate.
Leah comes to us from UCLA, where she was a Hedrick Assistant Adjunct Professor working with [Prof. Mason Porter](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~mason/), and before that completed her PhD at the [University of Limerick](https://www.ul.ie/) in the [Research Ireland Centre for Research Training in Foundations of Data Science](https://www.data-science.ie/).
Her work develops tools to understand how things spread on networks, with a particular interest in higher-order contagions.
Welcome Leah!
