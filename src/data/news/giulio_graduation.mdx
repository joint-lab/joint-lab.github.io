title: Giulio Burgio awarded Juan de la Cierva fellowship
date: 2025-06-25
categories: ['news']

We are excited to announce that [Giulio Burgio](../../people/giulio-burgio/), who has been a Postdoctoral Associate at the Vermont Complex Systems Institute with us, is moving to a new position at the [Alvaro Sanchez Lab](https://sanchezlaboratory.weebly.com/) at the Institute of Functional Biology and Genomics (IBFG), a joint research center between CSIC and the University of Salamanca in Spain.

During his time at UVM, Giulio made significant contributions to network science, focusing on spreading processes and higher-order interactions, including publications on [self-reinforcing cascades](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00714), [paradoxes in co-evolution of contagions and institutions](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rspb.2024.1117), and [adaptive hypergraphs and higher-order contagions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.11268).

Giulio will initially be supported by Dr. Sanchez's ERC funding through December 2025. Starting in January 2026, he will transition to a two-year position funded by the prestigious [Juan de la Cierva fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_la_Cierva_Scholarship), which he was recently awarded—one of Spain's most competitive research scholarships for outstanding young researchers.

Congratulations, Giulio, on this exciting move and the prestigious fellowship award!
