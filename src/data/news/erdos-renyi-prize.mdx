---

Huge news! Laurent Hébert-Dufresne has been awarded the [2026 Erdős–Rényi Prize](https://netscisociety.net/award-prizes/er-prize) by the Network Science Society.
It is the field's top honor for young researchers, recognizing exceptional contributions to network science.
The [announcement](https://netscisociety.net/news) highlights his "foundational contributions to the theory of contagion on complex networks, illuminating how network structure, higher-order interactions, and nonlinear transmission mechanisms shape the spread of diseases, behaviors, and ideas."
The award will be presented at [NetSci 2026](https://netscisociety.net/) this coming June.
Laurent follows in the footsteps of friend-of-the-lab [Antoine Allard, who won last year](https://dynamicalab.github.io/2024/06/21/ERprize.html). 
An incredible and very well-deserved recognition — congratulations Laurent!!
