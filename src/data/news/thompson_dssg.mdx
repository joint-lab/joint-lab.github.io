[**William Thompson**](../../people/will-thompson/) is spending this summer as a fellow in the [Data Science for Social Good (DSSG) Fellowship](https://dssgfellowship.org/), hosted this year by the [Bloomberg Center for Government Excellence at Johns Hopkins University](https://hub.jhu.edu/2026/02/19/data-science-for-social-good-fellowship-gov-ex/) in Baltimore, Maryland.
The program brings together graduate students for an intensive summer of building machine learning and data science projects with social impact.
We're excited to see William bridge the gap between theory and application during this program.
Congratulations Will!
