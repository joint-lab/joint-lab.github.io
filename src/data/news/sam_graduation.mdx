We are thrilled to announce that [Sam Rosenblatt](../../people/sam-rosenblatt/) has successfully defended his Ph.D. in Computer Science on July 18th. Sam’s dissertation, *“Pragmatic interventions against epidemics on networks”*, addressed crucial issues related to controlling contagion spread in complex systems, combining network science, agent-based models, and computational social science. As a [QuEST trainee](https://www.uvm.edu/quest), Sam’s interdisciplinary work has advanced our understanding of how interventions can be effectively designed under real-world constraints.

In addition to his research, Sam has been deeply involved in scientific communication and outreach, serving as the Outreach Officer and one of the founders of the [Society of Young Network Scientists (SYNS)](https://society-of-young-network-scientists.github.io/). His dedication to making science accessible and engaging has left a lasting impact on the lab and the broader scientific community. Sam is now a Research Scientist at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).

Congratulations, Sam, on this outstanding achievement! We look forward to seeing the difference you’ll make in your new role.
