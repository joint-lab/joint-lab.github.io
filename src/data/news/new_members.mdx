We are excited to welcome two new members to the lab!

[**Will Thompson**](../../people/will-thompson/), a Ph.D. student in Complex Systems & Data Science, joins us as a research assistant. Will’s research focuses on the role of randomness in statistical inference for copmlex systems.

[**Giulio Burgio**](../../people/giulio-burgio/), a SOCKS Postdoctoral Fellow, comes to us with a background in theoretical physics and complex systems. Giulio’s research explores the macroscopic behavior of complex networks, particularly in biological and socio-technical systems, with a focus on higher-order interactions and contagion processes.
