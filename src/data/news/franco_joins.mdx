We are excited to welcome [**João Franco**](../../people/joao-franco/) to the lab.
João is a Ph.D. student in Complex Systems & Data Science at UVM, joining us as a collaborator alongside the [Computational Ethics Lab](https://www.compethicslab.org/).
His research explores belief dynamics: how beliefs form and evolve, and how algorithmic, psychological, and social factors shape these processes.
Before Vermont, João worked with the [Social Physics & Complexity Lab](https://socialcomplexity.eu/) on an ERC-funded project investigating algorithmic biases in search engines and large language models.
Welcome João!
