---
title: Laurent Hébert-Dufresne appointed External Faculty at the Complexity Science Hub
date: 2026-02-24
categories: ['news']
---

Laurent Hébert-Dufresne has been appointed as [External Faculty at the Complexity Science Hub](https://csh.ac.at/laurent-hebert-dufresne/) (CSH) in Vienna, Austria. The CSH brings together researchers from around the world to advance the science of complex systems across disciplines.
