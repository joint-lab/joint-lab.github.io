---
title: Leah Keating moves to Dublin City University
date: 2026-09-01
categories: ['news']
---

It is with great excitement that we announce that [Leah Keating](../../people/leah-keating/) will join the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Dublin City University as an Assistant Professor on September 7.

Leah is leaving the lab after completing her time as a Postdoctoral Associate at UVM.
During her time in Vermont, she contributed---among many other things!---to our work on spreading processes in complex networks.
We are grateful for her time in the lab and wish her all the best as she returns to Ireland.

Congratulations, Leah, on this exciting new chapter!
