## Biography 

Jack D'Iorio is a masters student studying Complex Systems and Data Science at the University of Vermont, working within a core group of the [Vermont Complex Systems Institute](https://vermontcomplexsystems.org/). Before joining the Joint Lab, he completed an undergraduate degree here at the University of Vermont in Computer Science.
Jack's research interests lie with anything emergent about a computational model with a current focus on the dynamics of neural cellular automata, especially when they are constrained or challenged.
