## Biography

I am a Postdoctoral Associate interested in developing tools to help us understand how things spread on networks. The applications include the spread of infectious diseases, the spread of behavior in social networks, among many other applications.

Before joining UVM, I was a Hedrick Assistant Adjunct Professor at UCLA from July 2023 to June 2025, working with Prof. Mason Porter. Before that, I was a PhD student at the [University of Limerick, Ireland](https://www.ul.ie/) in the [Research Ireland Centre for Research Training in Foundations of Data Science](https://www.data-science.ie/). 

## Research interests
Network analysis for biology applications, mathematical modeling, disease dynamics, and probability generating functions.
