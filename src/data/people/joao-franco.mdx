João Franco is a PhD student in Complex Systems and Data Science at the University of Vermont, collaborating with The Joint Lab. He also works closely with the [Computational Ethics Lab](https://www.compethicslab.org/).
João's research interests lie in understanding the (complex) dynamics of social systems, with a current focus on belief dynamics — how beliefs form and evolve, and the impact of algorithmic, psychological and social factors on these processes.

Before coming to Vermont, João collaborated with the [Social Physics & Complexity Lab](https://socialcomplexity.eu/), led by [Prof. Joana Gonçalves de Sá](https://socialcomplexity.eu/team/joana-goncalves-de-sa/), on an ERC-funded project investigating algorithmic biases in search engines and large language models.
